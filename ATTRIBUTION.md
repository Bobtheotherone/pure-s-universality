# Mathematical provenance

The binary simulation in this paper stores an entire cyclic program period in each new frame. A contraction releases the selected word, and later contractions consume the letters in that copy. The numerical proof identifies the queue slices retained in the resulting tree. The following sources distinguish the ingredients of this construction.

The finite-menu register interpreter and the binary tape compiler use distinct numberings and output conventions. The Lean certificate for arbitrary natural output concerns the tape compiler. Its new proof identifies physical FIFO slices at a regular stop and proves that each accepted primitive observation has the same ground tree as a boundary observation. The first-hit bound for finite summaries applies even to a reader of the complete concrete registers.
