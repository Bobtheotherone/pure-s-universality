# Authors and contributions

**Radames Mercado-Barbosa and Anthony Langdon**

Mercado-Barbosa directed the pure-S universality project in which the periodic allocator, selected queue reduction, and register-machine construction were developed. The preceding manuscripts and source revisions are listed in [Mathematical provenance](ATTRIBUTION.md).

Langdon developed selected-port identities for finite word operations, the distinction between live and spent cells, and a finite reader that restarts at the root. His notes *The Root Remembers* and *From Copying to Recursion* describe these results. The word reader in Section 2 uses the live/spent convention. Earlier work in the Mercado-Barbosa project also records retained cells.

Cinematic Strawberry's earlier construction, directed by Alexander Filin, supplies the tape-to-tag compiler, numbering of deterministic machine tables, and terminal-row reader used in the numerical theorem. The 90 imported Lean modules are credited individually. [Third-party inventory](THIRD_PARTY_INVENTORY.json)

OpenAI Codex assisted with the mathematical derivations, Lean formalization, reference implementation, testing, and preparation of the manuscript.
