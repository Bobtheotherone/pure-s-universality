# Numerical certificate and verification record

The main numerical theorem is `UniformS.Numerical.universal_natural_io`. For all natural e, x and y it equates terminal output y of the decoded deterministic tape table with a regular hit and numerical readout y on its encoded ground term. The primitive analogue is `UniformS.Numerical.universal_single_natural_io`. Its guard is tested after every individual contextual contraction. The source numbering, accepted physical slices, graph equivalence and uniqueness are defined in [Numerical.lean](formal/PureS/Numerical.lean). The prior halting theorem and the two-channel Boolean-output theorem remain in the aggregate.
